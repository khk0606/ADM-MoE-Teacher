## Gate interpretation

`FULL_TRAIN_K3_PASS` requires pooled overall and High-Desk improvement,
case-win rates of at least 50%, non-worse pooled semantic violation, bounded
watch/write invariance and v5 replay, bounded results for all eight
scene/stratum groups, and at least two improving generations.

A pass locks the selected step-12 checkpoint and authorizes only an internal
development evaluation on `room_0201`. It does not authorize paper-test access.
The final paper test must use a separate independent source after the checkpoint
and evaluation protocol have been sealed.
